Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy describes how FrameTale: Slide Puzzle (also called FrameTale), an Android sliding-puzzle game, handles information. The game includes picture-puzzle collections (Adventure) and classic numbered sliding puzzles (Classic).

Responsible operator: Oleksandr Starovoit (operating under the public brand Pixel Cascade Studio)
Contact: gavkan.zeel@gmail.com
Address: Bodelschwinghstr. 59, 92224 Amberg, Germany

1. Information stored on your device

FrameTale saves gameplay information locally on your device using Unity's local storage, including PlayerPrefs. This may include completed levels, collected images, stars or records, an unfinished puzzle, sound and vibration preferences, and other game settings. There are no player accounts or cloud synchronization of progress in the current version. We do not run a separate server that stores your saved games.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it may permanently delete your local progress and preferences.

2. Advertising and third-party data

FrameTale integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to support banner advertisements, including adaptive banners during gameplay. Depending on SDK configuration, region and applicable privacy choices, Google and advertising partners may process information including IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This processing may support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where allowed, personalization.

We do not require your name, email address or a user account to play FrameTale. For Google's own practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Consent and privacy controls

Where required by law or platform rules, advertising consent is managed using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The consent interface may present options to agree, decline or manage choices. Whether a message appears can depend on your region, previous selections and the SDK's requirements. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising: permitted non-personalized or limited ads may still appear.

Where privacy options are required and available in the app, they can be reopened through the game's Settings screen. You may contact us at the address above with questions about advertising choices.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Local game information supports saved games and game preferences. To the extent such information constitutes personal data, our legal basis is the legitimate interest in providing a functional game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where consent is legally required for advertising technologies, including certain device storage or identifiers and personalized ads, the basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable ePrivacy rules). Certain security and fraud-prevention processing may have other lawful bases where permitted. Google explains its independent processing in the linked materials.

5. Sharing, transfers and retention

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google, its service providers and advertising partners, including in countries outside your own. Please consult Google's notices for transfer safeguards and retention practices. Game progress stored only on your device remains there until the app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled; we do not keep a server-side copy of that progress.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent. Contact gavkan.zeel@gmail.com regarding data we control. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. For data processed independently by Google, consult Google's privacy tools and notices.

7. Children and intended audience

FrameTale does not require users to submit their name, email address or contact details to play. The intended age groups and any child-directed designation will be stated in the Google Play listing when published. Advertising and consent configuration must be consistent with those store declarations and applicable requirements.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy if FrameTale's features, advertising partners or data practices change. Updates will be published here with a revised effective date.

1. Lokal auf dem Gerät gespeicherte Daten

FrameTale speichert Spielinformationen lokal auf Ihrem Gerät mit der lokalen Speicherung von Unity, einschließlich PlayerPrefs. Dazu können abgeschlossene Level, gesammelte Bilder, Sterne oder Rekorde, angefangene Partien, Ton- und Vibrationseinstellungen sowie weitere Spieloptionen gehören. In der aktuellen Version gibt es keine Spielerkonten oder Cloud-Synchronisierung des Fortschritts. Wir betreiben keinen separaten Server zur Speicherung Ihrer Spielstände.

Durch das Löschen der App-Daten oder die Deinstallation können lokale Spielstände und Einstellungen dauerhaft verloren gehen.

2. Werbung und Datenverarbeitung durch Dritte

FrameTale nutzt das Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) für Bannerwerbung, darunter adaptive Banner während des Spiels. Abhängig von SDK-Konfiguration, Region und Datenschutzeinstellungen können Google und Werbepartner Informationen wie IP-Adresse (die Rückschlüsse auf einen ungefähren Standort zulassen kann), Geräte- oder Werbekennungen, Interaktionen mit der App und Anzeigen sowie Diagnosedaten verarbeiten. Dies kann der Auslieferung und Messung von Werbung, der Betrugsprävention und — soweit zulässig — der Personalisierung dienen.

Zum Spielen sind weder die Eingabe von Name und E-Mail-Adresse noch ein Nutzerkonto erforderlich. Weitere Informationen finden Sie in der Datenschutzerklärung von Google und in den Datenschutzhinweisen zum Google Mobile Ads SDK.
